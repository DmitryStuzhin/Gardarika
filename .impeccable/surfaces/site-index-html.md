# Marketing site «Лист проекта» — surface brief

- Scope: the full marketing site in `site/` — home sheets, sixteen-house catalog (three new example houses plus thirteen existing bases), hash-routed project sheet, and a reserved slot for the future 3D calculator. The legacy calculator in `reference/` is not part of this surface.
- Mode: Persuade.
- Audience: Moscow-region families choosing a country house and a builder they can hold to a document.
- Job: see credible houses with real sizes, understand the order of work and what is fixed in the contract, then leave a request or wait for the constructor.
- Primary action: «Обсудить проект» / lead form; secondary: explore catalog; calculator slot announces the stage-by-stage constructor with PDF estimate.
- Proof available: 16 project bases with areas/dimensions (3 new ones are labeled examples with indicative prices), fixed estimate in contract, guarantee and terms written in contract (no numbers given), full cycle design–production–construction. No testimonials, counts, awards, years.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a bound set of working drawings for your house — every section a numbered sheet with frame, title block and dimension lines. It refuses the category default of full-bleed dream photo + cream cards + terracotta pill.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white drafting sheets on a pale grey table, blue-black ink hairlines, square corners everywhere, GOST-style title blocks, dimension lines with 45° ticks and millimetre values, material hatching (GOST 2.306), one committed Prussian-blue «синька» field for the constructor, and a red revision stamp used only for «пример» / «скоро» marks. Type: Geologica (sharp, slanted small caps for sheet labels), Golos Text for reading.

STORY: Visitor sees a real house measured to the millimetre, believes this builder thinks in documents (fixed estimate, contract terms), browses sheets of houses, and sends a request — or learns the constructor will assemble the house layer by layer into a PDF estimate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A framed sheet «Лист 01». Left 5/12: headline «Дом, собранный вокруг вашей жизни.», lede, ink CTA «Подобрать проект» + outline «Конструктор». Right 7/12: large house photo inside dimension lines (width top, height right, values in mm) switching between the three new houses; bottom-right title block with house name, area, floors, price «от», stage, «Лист 1 из 7». Sheet index nav across the top.

FORM: Architectural working-drawing set («Лист проекта»), my list position 1 (user-selected pick over assigned #3 «Годовой круг»), seed key 8ea785f5 (degraded roll, no challengers).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
