# Marketing site «Дом у леса» — surface brief

- Scope: the full marketing site in `site/` — home, sixteen-house photo catalog with filters, hash-routed project page, reserved slot for the future 3D calculator. The legacy calculator in `reference/` is not part of this surface.
- Mode: Persuade.
- Audience: Moscow-region families choosing a country house; they browse by photo, size and price and need to feel at home before they trust the builder.
- Job: find a house that fits the family, understand the order of work and what the contract fixes, leave a request or wait for the constructor.
- Primary action: «Обсудить дом» / lead form; catalog card → project page.
- Proof available: 16 house bases (3 new example houses with indicative prices, marked «Пример»), fixed estimate in contract, guarantee and terms written in contract (no numbers), full cycle. No testimonials, counts, awards, years.
- History: the first build («Лист проекта», drawing-set world, seed 8ea785f5) was rejected by the user as cold and not family-like, and the spec-table catalog as a bad catalog. The user chose «Дом у леса» from three offered directions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm, lived-in house site where photography of homes with families leads and the builder's precision shows up as small drafting details (dimension lines, title-block stamp, leaders). It refuses both the cold document look and the cream-and-terracotta template.

OWN-WORLD: Light misty ground with a green-grey bias, deep pine green as the owning color (constructor field, footer, contract panel), honey-amber for primary actions only, soft rounded media (20px) and pill buttons, gentle soft shadows under floating cards. Geologica headings, Golos Text for reading. Drafting details in pine ink: 45° ticked dimension lines, stamp grids, GOST hatches.

STORY: Visitor sees a real family at a real house, browses a photo catalog filtered by floors and size, opens a house with its sizes and plans, learns the contract fixes the estimate, and sends a request — or sees the stage-by-stage constructor coming.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: headline «Дом, собранный вокруг вашей жизни.», warm lede, honey CTA «Выбрать дом» + outline «Обсудить дом», three short true facts. Right (wider): large rounded photo of the three new houses switching, dimension line over it, white floating stamp card overlapping its lower-left corner with name, area, floors, price «от».

FORM: «Дом у леса», user-chosen from three offered directions after rejecting the drawing-set build; no new roll (seed of the original round 8ea785f5).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
